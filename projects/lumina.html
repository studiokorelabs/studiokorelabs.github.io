10 / FAQ

Before you
apply.

Who is LUMINA for?

Designers, artists, developers and creative professionals who already have a foundational practice and want to push into direction, spatial work, generative systems or creative technology.

Do I need to code?

No. Technical literacy is useful, but the programs are intentionally multidisciplinary. We teach the level of code and tooling required by each project.

How selective is admission?

We review applications for curiosity, craft, clarity of thinking and evidence of a personal point of view. A perfect portfolio is not required.

Can I work while studying?

Yes. The flagship cohort combines scheduled studio sessions with structured independent production.

Where is the academy?

Our primary studio is in Berlin, with selected sessions available remotely for the hybrid cohort.

What should I submit?

A concise portfolio, a short statement about your current practice and one project you wish you had made differently.
